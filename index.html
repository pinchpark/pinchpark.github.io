Safe by design

Pinch Park™ is built for people of all ages to have fun together.

  • Mute, Block and Report on every player.
  • Chat filtering on every device: bad words, phone numbers, links and social handles are hidden.
  • Kid-safe mode under 13: no voice chat, quick phrases only, no trading, and a parent check before purchases.
  • Voice your way: hear everyone nearby, friends only, or nobody.
  • Screen Time limits on multiplayer and messaging are respected.
  • No ads and no tracking. Delete your data any time from inside the game.
  • Comfort mode and break reminders for comfortable play.
  • Banned players are kept out of the Park and everyone's houses.
Health & safety: a very small number of people may have seizures from flashing lights or patterns. You can't see your room while playing: play seated or standing in one place, keep at least an arm's length of clear space around you (Table Tennis has you swing your arm), take regular breaks, and stop if you feel unwell. You play at your own risk. Read the full health and safety information before playing.
