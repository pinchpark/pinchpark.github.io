Safe for everyone.

Pinch Park is made for players of all ages, with safety built in from the start.

  • Mute, Block and Report on every player, any time.
  • Chat filtering hides bad words, phone numbers, links and social handles.
  • Kid-safe mode for under 13: no voice chat, quick phrases only, no trading, and a friendly nickname instead of their typed name.
  • A grown-up agrees to the rules for players under 18, and a parent check guards every purchase.
  • Voice your way: hear everyone nearby, friends only, or nobody.
  • Screen Time limits on multiplayer and messaging are respected.
  • No ads, no tracking. Delete your data from inside the game any time.
  • Comfort mode and break reminders for comfortable play.
Health & safety: a very small number of people may have seizures from flashing lights or patterns. You can't see your room while playing: play seated or standing in one place, keep at least an arm's length of clear space all around you (Table Tennis and Archery have you swing your arm or draw a bow), take regular breaks, and stop if you feel unwell. Children should play only with adult supervision. You play at your own risk. Read the full health and safety information before playing.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Pinch Park is free to download. You earn coins by playing, working shifts and winning games. Coin packs and the optional VIP Club are available as in-app purchases.

What do I need to play?

An Apple Vision Pro. You play with your hands: look and pinch. A game controller works too, if you have one.

Do I have to walk around my room?

No. Play seated or standing in one place: the game moves you. Just keep the space around you clear.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Meet up at the Park, invite friends to your house, or start a FaceTime game night with SharePlay. All 21 games can be played with friends, and you can practice against the computer any time.

Is it OK for kids?

Players under 13 automatically get kid-safe mode, and a parent or guardian agrees to the rules for anyone under 18. Apple recommends Apple Vision Pro for ages 13 and up, so younger children should play only with a parent's permission and supervision.
